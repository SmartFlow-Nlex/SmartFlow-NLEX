# Tollbound Heroes: asset briefs and manifest

Generated by `node scripts/tollbound-art/write-briefs.js` from the roster, the models and `manifest.json`. Do not edit by hand.

**How the art is made.** Every character is an original signed-distance clay model in `scripts/tollbound-art/scenes/`, ray-marched by `scripts/tollbound-art/render.js` and packed to WebP by `bake.js` (`node scripts/tollbound-art/bake.js`). Nothing is taken from another game or from stock art.

**Scale and pivot.** One model unit is about a third of a small car. `px/unit` is the bake scale; the pivot is where the sprite meets the ground, in sprite pixels from its top left. `wide` is the car width in model units; the road scales each hero so that width equals its real lane share, whatever the sprite size.

**Faces.** Eyes, lids, smile, blush and lamps are sculpted into the model (`scenes/sculpt.js`). `web/art/clayFaces.ts` adds only what moves, clipped to the lid openings the bake measured: iris, pupil and closing lids.

## Animation states (all characters, driven at run time)

| Who | State | How it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Idle | Front sprite, breathing bob, blink |
| Hero | Selection | Front sprite, hop and lean, `celebrate` face |
| Hero | Driving | `rear`, `mid`, then `turn` (mirrored for the other way) as it swings across, on a springy lean with a little squash; the cap trails the turn |
| Hero | Signature | Rear sprite plus the signature's own road effect; `determined` face in callouts |
| Hero | Hit | Short shake and tint; `hurt` face in callouts |
| Hero | Victory | Front sprite, `celebrate` face on the results screen |
| Sidekick | Idle | Front sprite with its own bob, speed, squash, tilt and sway (`RHYTHM`, `SWAY` in `web/art/clay.ts`) |
| Sidekick | Following | Rear sprite beside the car: flyers float, road ones hop; its tilt trails the car and what stands above the body sways |
| Sidekick | Assisting | Lift, squash and its own small flourish (`web/render/sidekickFx.ts`); the status tile turns gold with a diamond mark |
| Sidekick | Recovering | Status ring refills, tile desaturates, the dot goes away |
| Sidekick | Celebrating | `celebrate` face at reveals and results |
